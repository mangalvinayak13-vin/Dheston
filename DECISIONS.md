# DECISIONS

Every non-obvious choice with its reason. Newest last.

1. **Keep development 2016-2021 / test 2022-2026** instead of 70/30. It was fixed before any result existed and is more conservative on length of history; changing it now would be a tuning choice made after seeing results.
2. **Jump extension stays as the headline model.** The brief lists jumps as a stretch; the finished work shows jumps are what fix the fit (same-day error ~1.7 -> ~0.6 vol points, 2025-26). The brief's diffusion Double Heston is reported alongside as "DH2.0 diffusion".
3. **PINN targets the diffusion model.** The brief's PDE has no jump integral term. Jumps are priced by the exact Fourier pricer; a PINN with an integro-differential term is out of scope.
4. **No-arbitrage quote filters are diagnostics.** Removing violators changes every surface and would invalidate the finished walk-forward runs (~22 h of compute). The violation share is reported per year and a sensitivity run on 2025-26 shows the effect.
5. **New results go to `results/`; finished results stay where they are.** Avoids breaking `update_comparison.py` and the committed notes.
6. **DH1 baseline = 1.0's calibration style on the same clean data** (independent daily multi-start fit with the 1.0 pricer), measured on a stratified sample of test days (every 10th trading day) because a full independent refit of 1,173 days is ~3 hours; the same sampled days are used for every model in the attribution so comparisons are paired.
7. **Agents do not commit.** One reviewer (the main session) checks each output against its files, then commits per phase.
8. **The shipped surrogate is the data-trained network, not the physics-informed one** (work package B, measured, not chosen in advance). On real held-out chains the data-only network is more accurate on prices (0.049 vs 0.077 vol points mean) and the hybrid PINN is better on gamma, PDE residual and strike convexity, so the data-only model (`final.pt`) is used for calibration speed and the hybrid (`final_greeks.pt`) for Greeks. The PDE term did not improve data efficiency in the training budget, and physics-only training does not work as a pricer. Reported as found. Deviation from the spec: the gate multiplying the network is the Black vega plus a fat tail (it vanishes at tau=0, so the initial condition stays exact) instead of sqrt(tau), which was 3.5x worse in the same number of steps.
9. **The surrogate is valid only inside its training box** (tau 1 day to 0.6 years, |ln F/K| <= 0.30): 2.3% of real quotes have tau > 0.6 years and must use the exact pricer. Diffusion model only; it cannot price the jump model.
10. **Implied-vol errors of near-worthless options are not used as a metric.** For quotes whose model price is below about 0.01 rupee the exact pricer's own implied vol is ill-conditioned (a 1e-4 rupee price difference is several vol points); the calibration weights such quotes by max(vega, 0.05), so they carry almost no weight.
11. **The shared-slow-parameter design is not the source of the gain; the claim is withdrawn.** The attribution waterfall (118 paired test days, `results/attribution/`) shows the gain comes from the IV-space objective (-1.05 vol points next day), the global multi-start with the kappa ordering (-1.04) and Merton jumps (-1.66), and that adding the structure/state split makes the diffusion model slightly WORSE (+0.08, p = 0.003). Independent daily fits of the jump model forecast better (next-day 1.142) than the walk-forward structure/state jump model (1.254) on the same days. Yesterday's misfit adds -0.155. Earlier text describing the structural split as a key design element (README, comparison note, the original 2.0 motivation) must be read in this light; the split is kept only because the finished walk-forward results were produced with it, not because it is shown to help.
12. **The headline lead over "the best model-free benchmark" is stated against two benchmarks, and mostly concerns strikes yesterday did not quote.** Independent verification (`results/VERIFICATION.md` L1, re-derived and confirmed) showed the time-scaled smile repeats its edge value outside the range yesterday quoted (6.5% of test quotes). Inside the range the benchmark is slightly better than jump model + misfit (+0.012 vol points). A linear-wing benchmark (added after the verification, one variant, setting fixed in advance, `results/benchmark_check/`) closes 78% of the headline gap: the model's lead is -0.202 against the edge-value benchmark but **-0.045 [-0.061, -0.032]** against the linear-wing benchmark, ahead on 71% of days. All documents quote both. Not tested: an SVI or tuned wing benchmark.
